01.1

Why we're rebuilding the look

The current build reads as generic SaaS, not Ibadan: a pale mint header, thin grey-bordered inputs, a bold-but-characterless sans, and a green-to-yellow gradient reused on both the "Find Route" and "Contribute Route" buttons. Strip the wordmark and it could be any transit, fintech, or scheduling app. Nothing signals community, youth, or Nigeria — and the gradient is the clearest tell.

Before — live today

linear-gradient, green → yellow — soft, template-default, no edge

After — brand direction

flat chartreuse, charcoal outline — confident, poster-flat, no blend

The live mark today is a circular "RP" monogram in a green-to-teal gradient — the same generic-template problem as the buttons, one level up. 05

Illustration System

No mascot — a closed roster of recurring objects instead, so art direction stays a fixed checklist rather than a character needing a pose in every scene. Two render techniques, one rule set underneath.

01
Flat fills onlyOne or two colors max, pulled only from the core palette plus charcoal/cream. No gradients, ever — that's the one rule everything else exists to protect.
02
Consistent thick outlineEvery object gets the same relative stroke weight, heavy enough to read at thumbnail size on X.
03
Offset sticker-shadowA solid duplicate silhouette sits behind each object, same direction and distance every time — this single detail is what ties flat-sticker UI art to pixel badge art as one family.
04
Two techniques, one vocabularyUI and social content render flat and unframed. Badges render pixelated inside a shared rounded-square frame with a tier ring. Same object, same colors, different technique for different context.

06

Badge Roster

Mapped 1:1 to the six achievements already live on routepadi.com — nothing invented, nothing left over. Rendered as hand-authored pixel sprites (not smooth flat icons) — the retro/arcade texture the brief asked for, built from the same duotone palette as everything else.

First Ride
Unlocked on your first route submission
Object: keke — the vehicle of someone's very first logged journey.
Fare Whisperer
Submit 3 routes
Object: naira coin stack — knowing the real fare before you board.
Street Guardian
Submit 5 routes
Object: shield — safety and route trustworthiness.
Area Commander
Submit 10 routes
Object: flag on a pin — territory earned through volume.
City Explorer
Reach 2,500 XP
Object: compass — discovery earned through sustained contribution.
Master Scout
Reach 5,000 XP
Object: crown — the top tier, reserved for the highest XP bar.
Now designed and scheduled, not deferred anymore: the handshake badge for OG Padi is built — see Launch Campaign for the object and the rule that protects it. Deliberately kept out of the roster grid above: it's a one-time launch-window badge, not a repeatable achievement, and mixing it in with "submit 5 routes" style badges would blur that distinction.
Out of scope for this pass: the rank ladder (Route Scout → Route Commander), which is a single evolving title rather than a collectible, and the leaderboard's top-3 treatment — both intentionally deferred.
